- PLAY-31: Each frame uses two libplacebo devices. The engine renders YUV to RGBA on a headless device and reads it back to the CPU (`player-engine::video` `render_frame`); the window uploads that RGBA and composites overlays on its own device (`player-display::mac` `present_planes`). Both `--gpu-api` backends pay the readback. Render decoded planes straight into the window's swapchain on one device, with no CPU readback, and keep `--vo=null` and screenshots working.
- PLAY-32: VideoToolbox zero-copy on Metal: once PLAY-20 decodes through VideoToolbox, hand its `CVPixelBuffer` IOSurfaces to libplacebo with `pl_metal_wrap_iosurface` (in the carried Metal patch) instead of copying planes to the CPU. Depends on PLAY-20 and PLAY-31.
- PLAY-36: Setting `aid` to `no` does not stop audio. `App::disable_audio` (`crates/player-app/src/app.rs:198`) sets only the app-local `aid`/`aid_disabled`, while `EngineDemux::selected` keeps the first audio stream, so the scheduler keeps decoding and pushing it and `track-list` still reports it selected. The subtitle path does forward deselection (`crates/player-app/src/app.rs:217`), so the behaviour is asymmetric. Trigger: `set_property aid no` on a playing file and keep hearing it. `load_current` then resets `aid` to the first track while `aid_disabled` persists, so the report and the sound disagree in both directions. Add an engine-level audio-deselection (no selected stream, demux filters audio) called from `disable_audio` and cleared by select/auto, and reset the app flags together on load.
